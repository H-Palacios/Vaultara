2. Summary

  • Vaultara helps you keep track of when documents such as passports, ID cards, licences, insurance policies, vehicle licence discs and visas expire, and reminds you before they do.
  • We collect what you put into the app, plus the technical information needed to run your account securely.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not see or store your card details. Google Play and Apple handle payments.
  • Vaultara does not show adverts. If you allow usage data on Android, we also collect your device's advertising ID, only to measure how well our own adverts for Vaultara, shown on other websites and apps, lead to installs and purchases. You can say no, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • If you turn on end-to-end encryption for photos, the photos and the document numbers in your records are encrypted on your phone with a key only you hold. We cannot read them; we keep only the last four characters of each number so the app can show it masked.
  • Passport and ID scanning happens on your phone. The scan image is not sent to us for text recognition. If your phone cannot read a document with certainty, you can choose to have the text from the photo read online. The photo itself is never sent (see section 13.2).
  • Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States.
  • You can export your data in the app and delete your account at any time, in the app or at vaultara-f49ab.web.app/delete.

4. Information we collect

4.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, display name and, if you choose to add one, a profile photo.
  • Records: for each document or record you add, its name, category, expiry date, notes and, if you choose to add them, document numbers (for example, a passport or licence number). If you add them, also where you keep it (for example your wallet or bag), the phone number to call to block a lost card, the country that issued it, where a vehicle is registered or where a card or policy is held, the bank, insurer, store or company it is with, and a policy or document number. The number is never sent to the Ask Vaultara assistant.
  • Your categories and groups: the names of the categories and groups you create or rename, and the icon you pick for a category.
  • Attachments: photos and PDF files you choose to attach to a record. You can choose to encrypt photos on your device before they are uploaded (see section 12).
  • People in your household: the names of family or household members you add, and the records you enter for them.
  • Pets: each pet's name, its type (such as a dog, a rabbit or a type you write yourself), its date of birth if you add one, its microchip number and the registry the chip is listed with, and your vet's name and phone number if you add them. Pets are kept in their own list, apart from the people in your household, and the records you enter for a pet are linked to it. When you choose which pets come on a trip, Vaultara keeps that choice with the trip. Pet travel rules are worked out on your phone from the destination, the country you live in and the type of pet; nothing about your pets is sent to anyone else.
  • Pet photos and care: if you add a photo of a pet, it is stored in your account so that it shows on your other devices. Before it is uploaded, your phone makes it smaller and removes details such as where it was taken. The photo is deleted when you remove it or delete the pet. Care reminders (for example flea and tick treatment, deworming or a check-up) and the travel plan dates you choose to add are saved as ordinary records linked to the pet. A pet card you share as a PDF is made on your phone and goes only where you send it.
  • Pet type suggestions (paid plans): if you type a kind of animal that Vaultara does not know, only the word you typed (for example "tarantula") and your app language are sent to our servers to check whether it is an animal and to suggest a group and an icon (see section 13). The word is not linked to your account in what we keep: we save the answer under a scrambled (hashed) key so the same word is not checked twice. Words with numbers, links or symbols are refused on your phone and never sent.
  • Official travel rules: Vaultara's servers read the public pages of government websites, such as pet import and passport rules, about once a month, and keep the rules they find with the page address and the date. This uses no information about you. The same check can also note whether a country asks for a yellow fever certificate, whether visitors need an International Driving Permit, and its emergency number, each with the sentence it came from. Your phone downloads the rules for the countries you look at or travel to. If you ask Ask Vaultara about pet travel, it reads your pets and these rules to answer.
  • Trips: destinations, any cities or towns you add, travel dates, travellers, the reason for the trip, flights (where they leave from and land, dates and times) and the date you reach each country. For visa checks, the nationality and country of residence of each traveller.
  • Life event plans: the life event you pick (for example moving house, a new baby or moving to another country), its date if you add one, who it is for, the country you are moving to if you add one, which records and steps you marked as done, and any steps you type into a plan yourself. For a lost or stolen wallet, also whether it was lost or stolen, what went missing, the police case number if you add one, and the day it was found. To notice when you update a record, Vaultara also keeps a short code made from each related record when the plan starts. The code cannot be turned back into your record.
  • Staff details (Business plan): staff names, records and the email addresses you enter, or import from a spreadsheet you choose, so that we can send them reminders. When you import a staff list, the file is read on your phone and only the names and email addresses you add are stored; the file itself is not uploaded. Imported staff get reminder emails only if you choose that.
  • Shared links: the links you create to share a record, and what each link gives access to.
  • Calendar link: if you turn on calendar sync, we keep a private link that your calendar app reads, and the choices you make for it: which people and categories to include, whether to add "Renew soon" events and trips, and whether to hide record names. The calendar shows expiry dates, and, if you choose, trip names and dates. Anyone who has the link can read that calendar, so you can make a new link or turn it off at any time, and the old link stops working. Locked records never show their name in the calendar.
  • Change history (Business plan): when someone adds, changes, deletes or restores a shared record, we note who did it, when, the record's name and which fields changed. We never keep the old or new values, and for a locked record we keep no name. Only the business owner can see the history, and only while the account is on the Business plan.
  • Messages to the Ask Vaultara assistant (paid plans): the text you type or dictate to it, and the answers it gives. These are saved to your account as chat history so that you can come back to them. A card you have not confirmed yet (for example a new record or a renewal plan) is saved with its details for up to 7 days, so you can still confirm it later; once you confirm or cancel it, only a one-line summary is kept (see section 13). If you take a photo in the chat, only the text your phone reads from it is sent, never the photo.
  • Ratings and reports of assistant answers: if you mark an answer as helpful or not helpful, or report it, we save your rating or the reason for the report, the question you asked and the answer (see section 13).
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact us. When you email us from the app's Help screen, the message already contains your app version, phone model, system version, language and whether you are on a free or paid plan, so we can help without asking. It never contains your records, and you can delete those lines before sending. If you ask Ask Vaultara to email us, it writes the email for you and shows it first; it is sent only when you tap Confirm. That email contains your message, the same app details, your email address so that we can reply, your account's user ID and, if the problem is about one record, that record's name (never its details or document numbers). After Confirm you can choose to add one screenshot. Your phone saves the picture again first, which removes details such as where it was taken, and only a JPEG or PNG picture of up to 2 MB is accepted. Check that a screenshot shows no ID numbers, passwords or bank details before you add it.

4.2 Information created when you use Vaultara

  • Device list: for each device signed in to your account, the device model, operating system version, app version and when it was last seen.
  • Notification tokens: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for each device, which lets us send push notifications to it.

We keep a scrambled copy of the address for up to a day so these emails are not repeated too often.

  • Two-step sign-in: if you turn it on, the information needed to check codes from your authenticator app. If you make backup codes, we keep only a one-way scrambled (salted hash) copy of each code, so nobody can read the codes back from our records, and a code is deleted once it has been used or replaced.
  • Insights you put off: which insights you chose to see later and until when, so they stay hidden on all your devices for that week.
  • Pinned categories: which categories you pinned to the top of the Categories screen, so that the order is the same on all your devices.
  • Tutorial progress: which tutorials you finished, which chapters of the full tour you skipped and which "New here?" tips you closed, so that they are not shown again on your other devices.
  • Invitations: if you invite friends, your invite code and which accounts joined with it. If you join with someone's code, the code you used, who invited you and the free Premium months each of you received. To stop the same person claiming the invite reward twice, we also keep a one-way scrambled (hashed) copy of the verified email address that claimed it. It cannot be turned back into your email address.
  • Consent records: what you agreed to (for example, this policy, the Terms of Service or analytics) and when.
  • Analytics and advertising measurement: information about how the app is used, if analytics is turned on, and on Android your device's advertising ID, if you allow it (see section 14).
  • App integrity checks: Firebase App Check confirms that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a genuine device. To do this it uses Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iOS, with DeviceCheck used on devices that do not support App Attest.

4.3 Your approximate location (optional)

This is off unless you turn on Use my location for trips in Security and privacy, or allow it when Ask Vaultara asks. It lets Vaultara tell whether you are at home or travelling when you ask about trips.

  • Approximate only: we ask your phone for approximate location, not precise location, and only while the app is open. Vaultara never uses your location in the background.
  • Only the country is used: your phone turns the position into a country on the device. To do this, your phone's operating system may use its own location and geocoding services (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) under their privacy terms. Your coordinates are not sent to us.
  • When it is sent: only the two letter country code (for example "JP") is sent, and only together with a message you send to Ask Vaultara, so that the assistant can answer (see section 13).
  • Not stored: we do not save your location or the country in your account, and we do not keep a location history. The app keeps the country in memory for up to 15 minutes so that it does not ask your phone for every message.
  • Your choice: you can turn it off at any time in Security and privacy, or remove the permission in your phone's settings. Everything else in Vaultara works without it.

4.4 Information from Google Play and Apple

When you buy or renew a subscription, the store tells us which plan you bought, the billing period, which store you used, and the relevant dates. We keep this as your billing history. We do not receive your card number or bank details.

5. Information we do not collect

  • Payment card details. Google Play and Apple are the merchants of record and process all payments.
  • The contents of encrypted photos and document numbers. If you use end-to-end encryption, we store only the encrypted file or number (and a number's last four characters). We do not have the key.
  • Scan images for text recognition. Passport, ID and barcode scanning uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. The camera image is processed on the device. Only the details you choose to save are stored in your account. Scanning several documents at once (Premium) works the same way: each photo is read on your phone, nothing is sent online, and nothing is saved until you check the list and add the records; those photos are not attached or kept. If you also attach the photo to a record, it is uploaded like any other attachment. If your phone cannot read a document with certainty and you agree, the text it recognised is sent to Anthropic to be read, without the machine readable code lines and with long numbers hidden. The image itself is never sent (see section 13.2).
  • Precise location or location history. If you turn on Use my location for trips, only the country you are in is used, and it is not stored (see section 4.3).
  • Biometric data. If you use fingerprint or face unlock for the app lock, your phone's operating system checks it. Vaultara and Vorkalis never receive your fingerprint or face data.

When you add records for family or household members, you are usually doing so for personal or household purposes. If you add information about another adult, please make sure they know and agree. If you share access with them through the Family plan, they can see this policy in the app.

If you use the Business plan to track your staff's documents, your business decides what staff information is stored and why. For that information, your business is the responsible party (or controller) and Vorkalis acts as an operator (or processor) on your behalf. This means:

Staff who receive reminder emails from Vaultara can contact us at privacy@vorkalis.com if they have questions.

For transfers from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where it applies), which are included in Google's and Anthropic's data processing terms. Where a provider is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, that certification may also apply. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

12. How we protect information

Section 19 of POPIA and Article 32 of the GDPR require us to take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information. These are the main steps we take:

  • Encryption: information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest on Google's servers.
  • End-to-end encrypted photos (optional): photos are encrypted on your device with a key that only you hold. We cannot read them. If you lose your key, we cannot recover these photos for you.
  • Two-step sign-in: you can require a code from an authenticator app when you sign in. Backup codes let you sign in if you lose your phone; using one turns two-step sign-in off, sends you a security alert and asks you to set it up again.
  • Sign-in protection: a wrong email or password always gets the same message, so the sign-in screen does not reveal whether an account exists, and repeated wrong attempts are slowed down.
  • App lock: you can lock the app with your phone's fingerprint or face unlock.
  • Screenshot blocking: the app blocks screenshots and screen recording on sensitive screens.
  • Sign-in alerts: we send you a security alert email when your account is signed in to on a device we have not seen before. We also send an alert when your password is changed, when two-step sign-in is turned off, when someone asks to change your email address, and when all other devices are signed out.
  • Device list and remote sign-out: you can see every device signed in to your account and sign any of them out.
  • App Check: requests to our servers must come from a genuine copy of the app.
  • Strict database rules: our database and storage rules allow each person to reach only the information they are allowed to see.
  • Limited access: only people at Vorkalis who need access to run the service can access personal information, and they must keep it confidential.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password, turn on two-step sign-in, and keep your phone locked.

13. The Ask Vaultara assistant

Ask Vaultara is an optional assistant available on paid plans. It uses an AI model provided by Anthropic.

  • Nothing from your records or chats is sent to Anthropic unless you use the assistant. The only other things Vaultara sends to Anthropic are the name of a new category and the words you type into the icon search, for icon suggestions (see section 13.1), a kind of animal you type that Vaultara does not know, with your app language, to check it and suggest a group (see section 4.1), and, if you agree, the text of a document your phone could not read (see section 13.2).
  • When you use it, your messages in the current chat are sent to Anthropic so that it can produce an answer. If you continue a saved chat, its earlier messages are sent too.
  • If you use the camera button in the chat, your phone reads the text on the photo itself. Only that text is sent to Anthropic, with long numbers such as document numbers hidden first. The photo is never sent; it stays on your phone and is added to the record only if you confirm the new record.
  • When you ask how to renew something, the assistant can search the web for official information, using Anthropic's web search tool. Only general search words are used, such as the kind of document and the countries; your name, your records' own names and document numbers are not put into searches. The pages it used are shown under the answer as links.
  • If you confirm a renewal plan, its steps, links, fee, processing time and any appointment you save (date, time, place and what to bring) are stored in your account so they appear on your other devices. You can remove an appointment at any time, and plans are deleted with your account. Appointment reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • In Talk mode, your phone turns your voice into text and reads the answers aloud with its own voices. Vaultara receives only the text, never a recording, and no audio is sent to Anthropic.
  • When you correct the assistant about which record you mean (for example "green book" for your passport), it can save that word and the record it points to in your account, so that it understands you next time. Up to 100 words are kept. You can clear them at any time with Forget learned words in Chat history, and they are deleted with your account.
  • To answer your question, the assistant can look up your information in Vaultara and send it to Anthropic. This can include the names, groups, categories, expiry dates, notes, renewal costs and reminder settings of your records and of records shared with your household, the names of the people you track records for, the passport countries and country of residence you saved for yourself and for them, your trips (dates, countries, cities or towns, travellers, reason for the trip, flights and arrival dates), and which plan you have. When you ask about pet travel, it can also read your pets, their records and the travel date you picked, to work out a plan. On the Business plan, the owner can ask it about the change history of shared records. It does not look up document numbers, attachments or pet photos. For locked records, it sees only that a locked record exists and its expiry date, not its name, group or notes.
  • If you turned on Use my location for trips, the country your phone is in (for example "JP") is sent with each message, so that the assistant can tell whether you are at home or travelling. Your coordinates are never sent (see section 4.3).
  • The assistant cannot save or change anything by itself. If it suggests a change, for example a new record, a new renewal cost, a deleted record, a person or a trip, nothing changes until you confirm it in the app.
  • Your chats are saved to your account as chat history, so that you can come back to them and continue them. A saved chat holds your messages, the answers, and a short summary of each change the assistant suggested and whether you confirmed it.
  • Saved chats are deleted automatically 90 days after their last message. You can delete any chat, or all chats, at any time in Ask Vaultara under Chat history. When you delete your account, your saved chats are deleted with it.
  • Anthropic deletes the messages and answers it processes for us within 30 days by default, unless it needs to keep them longer for trust and safety reasons or because the law requires it.
  • Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models by default.
  • Do not send end-to-end encrypted photos or anything you would not want processed by Anthropic. Only send what you need for your question.
  • Answers can be wrong. Always confirm important information with an official source.
  • When the assistant cannot do something you ask, for example because Vaultara has no such feature, it notes privately a general description of what was asked, such as "export records to Excel", in a monthly count. It also counts, by name only, the assistant's tools that did not work. These counts contain no user ID, no message text and no names, numbers or dates, so they cannot identify you. We use them only to decide what to improve.
  • If you ask for help with a problem in the app, the assistant first suggests the fix it knows. It offers to email us if that does not help, or straight away for matters such as a wrong charge or a locked account (see Support messages in section 4.1).
  • When you dictate a message, your phone's own speech recognition turns your voice into text (Google on Android, Apple on iOS, under their privacy terms). Vaultara receives only the text, never the recording.
  • Under each answer you can mark it as helpful or not helpful, or report it with the flag button if it is offensive, harmful, wrong or misleading. Your rating or report is saved to your account with the question and the answer, so that we can review it and improve the assistant. For a report, we also receive an email with the reason, the question, the answer and your account's user ID, but not your name or email address. Ratings and reports are deleted automatically after 90 days, and we keep the report email only for as long as we need it to review the answer.

13.1 Icon suggestions for categories

When you make your own category, Vaultara picks an icon for it from the name you type, on all plans. When you change a category's icon, it suggests a few icons for the name and lets you search for one in your own words.

  • The app first makes a guess on your phone. A moment later, the category name or the search words you typed (up to 40 characters) are sent through our servers to Anthropic's AI model, which picks the best icons from a fixed list of about 200.
  • Only that text is sent to Anthropic. No records, people, dates, document numbers or photos are sent, and your account is not identified to Anthropic.
  • We do not save the text with the request. The name is saved in your account only when you create the category, together with the icon. Search words are never saved.
  • You can change the icon at any time. If you do not want the text sent, do not type anything you would not want processed by Anthropic; the same deletion and training terms apply as for the assistant above.

13.2 Reading documents that your phone could not read

Snap to add reads a photo of a document on your phone with Google ML Kit, on all plans. Passports, ID cards with machine readable code lines and vehicle licence discs are read and checked on your phone. For other documents, or when the check fails, your phone may not be able to tell which date is the expiry date or what the document is.

  • In that case, and only after you agree the first time, the text your phone recognised is sent through our servers to Anthropic's AI model, which says what the document is, which country issued it and which printed date is the expiry date.
  • The photo is never sent. Before the text is sent, the app removes the machine readable code lines and replaces every long number, such as ID, passport, policy, account and card numbers, with # characters. Dates, names and other words on the document are sent as they appear.
  • We do not save the text with the request and do not log it. Only the details you choose to save in the record are stored in your account.
  • The answer only fills in the form. You check it before saving, and an expiry date is used only if it is printed in the text.
  • If you do not agree, or have no internet connection, the app keeps what your phone read. The same deletion and training terms apply as for the assistant above.

14. Analytics and advertising measurement

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how people use Vaultara, for example which screens are used and where the app has problems. We do not use analytics to see the contents of your records.

When something goes wrong in the app, an error report is sent through Firebase Analytics, only if analytics is on. It contains only a short name for the place in the app where the error happened (for example "records_stream") and the type of error. It never contains the error message, your records, your name or anything you typed.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, analytics is off until you choose to turn it on.
  • Everywhere else, analytics is on by default until you turn it off. The app decides which applies from the country and region settings of your phone.
  • You can turn analytics on or off at any time in the app under Profile, then Security and privacy, then Share usage data.

Firebase Analytics uses an app instance identifier and collects information such as device type, operating system, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address, the screens you open and the events you trigger in the app. The events we log are things like adding, deleting or restoring a record, setting a reminder, adding a group or category, finishing a tutorial and viewing the upgrade screen. Some events include the type of category used (for example "passport"), but never the name, notes, dates or document numbers of your records. We do not link analytics to your account or send your user ID to Google Analytics.

14.1 Advertising ID (Android)

The advertising ID is a random number that your Android phone gives to apps for advertising and measurement. It does not contain your name or email address, and you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (usually under Privacy, then Ads).

  • What we use it for: only to measure how well our own adverts for Vaultara, shown on Google services and on other websites and apps, lead to installs and purchases of a Vaultara plan. For this, Firebase Analytics is linked to Google Ads, and both are provided by Google.
  • What we do not do: Vaultara does not show adverts. We do not use the advertising ID to personalise adverts, we turn ad personalisation off, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with your records, name or email address.
  • Your choice, in every country: we collect the advertising ID only after you have chosen to allow usage data, either by tapping Allow on the "Help improve Vaultara" screen or by turning on Share usage data in the app. If you do not allow it, or you turn it off later, the app stops using it. You can also delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Who receives it: Google, as our service provider (see section 9). Google receives the advertising ID together with the analytics events described above, and never the contents of your records.

16. Your rights

Under POPIA (sections 5, 23, 24 and 11(3)) and, where they apply, the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: ask whether we hold information about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correction: correct information that is wrong or incomplete. You can edit most information directly in the app.
  • Export (data portability): download your data in the app using the export feature.
  • Deletion: delete your information or your whole account (see section 17).
  • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Restriction: under the GDPR, ask us to limit how we use your information in some situations.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.
  • Not be subject to automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (see section 18).
  • Complain to a supervisory authority (see section 20).

How to use your rights

Many of these rights are available directly in the app. For anything else, email our Information Officer at privacy@vorkalis.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on a request, so that we do not give your information to someone else.

We will respond within one month, or sooner if the law requires. If a request is complex, we may need more time, and we will tell you why. We do not charge for using the in-app export or for reasonable requests. Requests for access to records may also be made under the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (PAIA), as described in our PAIA manual, which is available on request at privacy@vorkalis.com.

17. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • in the app, under Profile, then Security and privacy, then Delete account, or
  • on the web, at https://vaultara-f49ab.web.app/delete, even if you no longer have the app installed.

Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

You can delete parts of your data at any time in the Vaultara app, without deleting your account:

  • a record: open the record, tap the menu, then Delete;
  • a photo or PDF: open the record, tap the file, then Delete;
  • a family member and their records: Profile, then Family, open the person, then Delete;
  • a trip: open the trip, tap the menu, then Delete;
  • a life event plan: open the plan, then Delete plan;
  • a shared link: Profile, then Shared links, then Stop sharing;
  • an Ask Vaultara chat: open Ask Vaultara, tap Chat history, then delete the chat, or choose Delete all chats;
  • your profile photo: Profile, tap your photo, then Remove.

Deleted records, groups and categories first move to Recently deleted in the Recovery Centre, so that you can bring them back if you deleted them by mistake. They are deleted permanently after 30 days, the next time you open the app, or straight away if you delete them from Recently deleted. Other items are removed from our database and storage straight away. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time, as described in section 11. If you cannot find an option, contact us at support@vorkalis.com and we will delete the data for you.

When you delete your account, we delete your database records and all files in storage linked to your account. If part of the deletion fails, our system retries until it is complete. Some information may remain for a limited time in backups and in the records described in section 11.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play or the App Store, otherwise the store may continue to charge you.

If you are the owner of a Family plan, deleting your account ends access for your household members. If you are a member, deleting your account removes your own data but not the owner's.
