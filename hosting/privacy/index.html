2. Summary

  • Vaultara helps you keep track of when documents such as passports, ID cards, licences, insurance policies, vehicle licence discs and visas expire, and reminds you before they do.
  • We collect what you put into the app, plus the technical information needed to run your account securely.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not see or store your card details. Google Play and Apple handle payments.
  • Vaultara does not show adverts. If you allow usage data on Android, we also collect your device's advertising ID, only to measure how well our own adverts for Vaultara, shown on other websites and apps, lead to installs and purchases. You can say no, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • If you turn on end-to-end encryption for photos, the photos are encrypted on your phone with a key only you hold. We cannot read them.
  • Passport and ID scanning happens on your phone. The scan image is not sent to us for text recognition.
  • Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States.
  • You can export your data in the app and delete your account at any time, in the app or at vaultara-f49ab.web.app/delete.

4. Information we collect

4.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, display name and, if you choose to add one, a profile photo.
  • Records: for each document or record you add, its name, category, expiry date, notes and, if you choose to add them, document numbers (for example, a passport or licence number).
  • Attachments: photos and PDF files you choose to attach to a record. You can choose to encrypt photos on your device before they are uploaded (see section 12).
  • People in your household: the names of family or household members you add, and the records you enter for them.
  • Trips: destinations, travel dates and travellers. For visa checks, the nationality and country of residence of each traveller.
  • Staff details (Business plan): staff names, records and the email addresses you enter so that we can send them reminders.
  • Shared links: the links you create to share a record, and what each link gives access to.
  • Messages to the Ask Vaultara assistant (paid plans): the text you type or dictate to it, and the answers it gives. These are saved to your account as chat history so that you can come back to them (see section 13). You cannot send images or files to the assistant.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact us.

4.2 Information created when you use Vaultara

  • Device list: for each device signed in to your account, the device model, operating system version, app version and when it was last seen.
  • Notification tokens: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for each device, which lets us send push notifications to it.
  • Security history: sign-in events, including the device and a partially masked IP address (part of the address is hidden before we store it).
  • Two-step sign-in: if you turn it on, the information needed to check codes from your authenticator app.
  • Consent records: what you agreed to (for example, this policy, the Terms of Service or analytics) and when.
  • Analytics and advertising measurement: information about how the app is used, if analytics is turned on, and on Android your device's advertising ID, if you allow it (see section 14).
  • App integrity checks: Firebase App Check confirms that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a genuine device. To do this it uses Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iOS, with DeviceCheck used on devices that do not support App Attest.

4.3 Information from Google Play and Apple

When you buy or renew a subscription, the store tells us which plan you bought, the billing period, which store you used, and the relevant dates. We keep this as your billing history. We do not receive your card number or bank details.

5. Information we do not collect

  • Payment card details. Google Play and Apple are the merchants of record and process all payments.
  • The contents of encrypted photos. If you use end-to-end encryption, we store only the encrypted file. We do not have the key.
  • Scan images for text recognition. Passport, ID and barcode scanning uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. The camera image is processed on the device. Only the details you choose to save are stored in your account. If you also attach the photo to a record, it is uploaded like any other attachment.
  • Biometric data. If you use fingerprint or face unlock for the app lock, your phone's operating system checks it. Vaultara and Vorkalis never receive your fingerprint or face data.

10. Transfers outside South Africa

Your information is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States. If you use the Ask Vaultara assistant, your messages and the record details it looks up are also sent to Anthropic in the United States.

Under POPIA

Section 72 of POPIA allows us to transfer personal information to another country if certain conditions are met. We rely on:

  • Binding agreements: our agreements with Google and Anthropic (their data processing terms) require them to protect your information to a standard that is substantially similar to POPIA, including limits on how they may use it and duties to keep it secure (section 72(1)(a)).
  • Performance of our agreement with you: storing your data in the United States is necessary to provide the Vaultara service you signed up for (section 72(1)(c)).
  • Your consent: when you create an account, we ask you to agree to this transfer (section 72(1)(b)).

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR

For transfers from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where it applies), which are included in Google's and Anthropic's data processing terms. Where a provider is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, that certification may also apply. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

12. How we protect information

Section 19 of POPIA and Article 32 of the GDPR require us to take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information. These are the main steps we take:

  • Encryption: information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest on Google's servers.
  • End-to-end encrypted photos (optional): photos are encrypted on your device with a key that only you hold. We cannot read them. If you lose your key, we cannot recover these photos for you.
  • Two-step sign-in: you can require a code from an authenticator app when you sign in.
  • App lock: you can lock the app with your phone's fingerprint or face unlock.
  • Screenshot blocking: the app blocks screenshots and screen recording on sensitive screens.
  • Sign-in alerts: we send you a security alert email when your account is signed in to on a device we have not seen before. We also send an alert when your password is changed, when two-step sign-in is turned off, when someone asks to change your email address, and when all other devices are signed out.
  • Device list and remote sign-out: you can see every device signed in to your account and sign any of them out.
  • App Check: requests to our servers must come from a genuine copy of the app.
  • Strict database rules: our database and storage rules allow each person to reach only the information they are allowed to see.
  • Limited access: only people at Vorkalis who need access to run the service can access personal information, and they must keep it confidential.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password, turn on two-step sign-in, and keep your phone locked.

13. The Ask Vaultara assistant

Ask Vaultara is an optional assistant available on paid plans. It uses an AI model provided by Anthropic.

  • Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you use the assistant.
  • When you use it, your messages in the current chat are sent to Anthropic so that it can produce an answer. If you continue a saved chat, its earlier messages are sent too. You cannot send images or files to the assistant.
  • To answer your question, the assistant can look up your information in Vaultara and send it to Anthropic. This can include the names, groups, categories, expiry dates, notes, renewal costs and reminder settings of your records and of records shared with your household, the names of the people you track records for, your trips (dates, countries and travellers), and which plan you have. It does not look up document numbers or attachments. For locked records, it sees only that a locked record exists and its expiry date, not its name, group or notes.
  • The assistant cannot save or change anything by itself. If it suggests a change, for example a new record, a new renewal cost, a deleted record, a person or a trip, nothing changes until you confirm it in the app.
  • Your chats are saved to your account as chat history, so that you can come back to them and continue them. A saved chat holds your messages, the answers, and a short summary of each change the assistant suggested and whether you confirmed it.
  • Saved chats are deleted automatically 90 days after their last message. You can delete any chat, or all chats, at any time in Ask Vaultara under Chat history. When you delete your account, your saved chats are deleted with it.
  • Anthropic deletes the messages and answers it processes for us within 30 days by default, unless it needs to keep them longer for trust and safety reasons or because the law requires it.
  • Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models by default.
  • Do not send end-to-end encrypted photos or anything you would not want processed by Anthropic. Only send what you need for your question.
  • Answers can be wrong. Always confirm important information with an official source.

14. Analytics and advertising measurement

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how people use Vaultara, for example which screens are used and where the app has problems. We do not use analytics to see the contents of your records.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, analytics is off until you choose to turn it on.
  • Everywhere else, analytics is on by default until you turn it off. The app decides which applies from the country and region settings of your phone.
  • You can turn analytics on or off at any time in the app under Profile, then Security and privacy, then Share usage data.

Firebase Analytics uses an app instance identifier and collects information such as device type, operating system, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address, the screens you open and the events you trigger in the app. The events we log are things like adding, deleting or restoring a record, setting a reminder, adding a group or category, finishing a tutorial and viewing the upgrade screen. Some events include the type of category used (for example "passport"), but never the name, notes, dates or document numbers of your records. We do not link analytics to your account or send your user ID to Google Analytics.

14.1 Advertising ID (Android)

The advertising ID is a random number that your Android phone gives to apps for advertising and measurement. It does not contain your name or email address, and you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (usually under Privacy, then Ads).

  • What we use it for: only to measure how well our own adverts for Vaultara, shown on Google services and on other websites and apps, lead to installs and purchases of a Vaultara plan. For this, Firebase Analytics is linked to Google Ads, and both are provided by Google.
  • What we do not do: Vaultara does not show adverts. We do not use the advertising ID to personalise adverts, we turn ad personalisation off, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with your records, name or email address.
  • Your choice, in every country: we collect the advertising ID only after you have chosen to allow usage data, either by tapping Allow on the "Help improve Vaultara" screen or by turning on Share usage data in the app. If you do not allow it, or you turn it off later, the app stops using it. You can also delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Who receives it: Google, as our service provider (see section 9). Google receives the advertising ID together with the analytics events described above, and never the contents of your records.

16. Your rights

Under POPIA (sections 5, 23, 24 and 11(3)) and, where they apply, the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: ask whether we hold information about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correction: correct information that is wrong or incomplete. You can edit most information directly in the app.
  • Export (data portability): download your data in the app using the export feature.
  • Deletion: delete your information or your whole account (see section 17).
  • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Restriction: under the GDPR, ask us to limit how we use your information in some situations.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.
  • Not be subject to automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (see section 18).
  • Complain to a supervisory authority (see section 20).

How to use your rights

Many of these rights are available directly in the app. For anything else, email our Information Officer at privacy@vorkalis.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on a request, so that we do not give your information to someone else.

We will respond within one month, or sooner if the law requires. If a request is complex, we may need more time, and we will tell you why. We do not charge for using the in-app export or for reasonable requests. Requests for access to records may also be made under the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (PAIA), as described in our PAIA manual, which is available on request at privacy@vorkalis.com.

17. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • in the app, under Profile, then Security and privacy, then Delete account, or
  • on the web, at https://vaultara-f49ab.web.app/delete, even if you no longer have the app installed.

Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

You can delete parts of your data at any time in the Vaultara app, without deleting your account:

  • a record: open the record, tap the menu, then Delete;
  • a photo or PDF: open the record, tap the file, then Delete;
  • a family member and their records: Profile, then Family, open the person, then Delete;
  • a trip: open the trip, tap the menu, then Delete;
  • a shared link: Profile, then Shared links, then Stop sharing;
  • an Ask Vaultara chat: open Ask Vaultara, tap Chat history, then delete the chat, or choose Delete all chats;
  • your profile photo: Profile, tap your photo, then Remove.

Deleted items are removed from our database and storage straight away. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time, as described in section 11. If you cannot find an option, contact us at support@vorkalis.com and we will delete the data for you.

When you delete your account, we delete your database records and all files in storage linked to your account. If part of the deletion fails, our system retries until it is complete. Some information may remain for a limited time in backups and in the records described in section 11.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play or the App Store, otherwise the store may continue to charge you.

If you are the owner of a Family plan, deleting your account ends access for your household members. If you are a member, deleting your account removes your own data but not the owner's.
