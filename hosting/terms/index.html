5. What Vaultara does and does not do

Vaultara helps you keep track of the expiry dates of documents and records, such as passports, ID cards, licences, insurance policies, vehicle licence discs and visas, for yourself, your family or household, and, on the Business plan, your staff. It reminds you before they expire and gives you related information.

Vaultara is a reminder and organising tool. It is not a government service and does not renew, verify or replace any document. In particular:

  • You remain responsible for renewing your documents on time and for checking that the dates and details you enter are correct.
  • Reminders depend on your phone, its settings, your internet connection, and services run by others (such as Google, Apple and email providers). A reminder may arrive late or not at all.
  • Information in the app, including visa information and answers from the Ask Vaultara assistant, is general information and not legal, immigration, insurance or financial advice.

We will take reasonable care in providing Vaultara, as the CPA requires (see section 20).
